      PULSE

      PULSE exists for one reason: to give home-visit providers back the hours they lose to bad routes. Every extra mile driven is a patient not seen — PULSE's job is to make sure the day's drive time serves the schedule, not the other way around.

      Why PULSE exists

      Picture the drive: a provider heads out to see a patient, passes within two minutes of another patient on her list, but has to keep going — because that patient wasn't in "today's group." An hour later, she's driving right back past that same street to catch someone else. Multiply that by every working day, every provider, every month. The miles add up to hours. The hours add up to patients who could have been seen but weren't.

      That's the exact problem PULSE was built to end. Not a generic map, not a spreadsheet of addresses sorted by hand — a system that actually understands geography the way a driver does: cluster what's close, route what's efficient, and never force a detour just to satisfy a label on a list. Real roads, real drive times, real math — so the only thing standing between a provider and more patients seen each day is how many hours she has, not how badly the route was planned.

      That's the pulse of it — less time on the road, more time at the door.

      What PULSE does

      🗂️

      Organizes your patients

      Upload a CSV once. PULSE geocodes every address and automatically clusters patients into tight, walkable groups — no manual sorting, no spreadsheets.

      🗺️

      Builds the smartest route

      Pick a group and how many stops you want. PULSE finds the closest real-road route using actual driving directions — not guesses — and shows you exactly when you'll arrive home.

      📅

      Remembers everything

      Every approved day lands on a calendar you can look back on, drag to reschedule, or export straight into your EHR system.

      🔔

      Watches the 30-day clock

      PULSE tracks who's due for a visit and flags anyone approaching or past 30 days since their last one — so nobody falls through the cracks.

      How a day works

      1. Upload your patient list in Client Center. 2. Check groups on the Clients tab — PULSE clusters automatically, or draw your own. 3. Build a route on the Schedule tab — pick a group, set your stop count, generate. 4. Approve the day when it looks right, and it lands on your Calendar. 5. Export whenever you need a clean list for billing or your EHR.

      A note on how the numbers work

      Drive times and the route line come from a live road-routing service — real streets, real turns, not straight-line guesses. If that service is ever briefly unreachable, PULSE automatically falls back to a straight-line estimate and tells you clearly when it has, so you always know which kind of number you're looking at.
